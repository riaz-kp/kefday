01 Our Story

Kef /kef/ · noun
the quiet delight of an unhurried moment.

Kefday began with a simple belief — that a café can be a small, daily ritual of happiness.

For four years, Areekode has gathered at our tables: for the first coffee at dawn, wings at midnight, and the songs in between. We brew with care, cook with generosity, and keep the lights on for whoever walks in next. Every cup, every plate, every evening of live music is our way of keeping a culture of happiness alive.
